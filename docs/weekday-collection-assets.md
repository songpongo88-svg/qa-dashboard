# 7 Days Collection

The seven photorealistic workspace scenes and the matching 4 × 4 transparent prop atlases were generated for this QA Dashboard and approved on 20 October 2026. The original approved images were recovered without changing their artwork. Public assets use WebP, each below 700,000 bytes. Scenes are 1672 × 941; transparent atlases are 1254 × 1254.

| Day | Scene | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Golden Morning | Yellow daisies, ivory and champagne |
| Tuesday | Blush Studio | Tulips, pink ceramic and rose gold |
| Wednesday | Botanical Focus | Birch, foliage and green glass |
| Thursday | Amber City | City sunset, amber and copper |
| Friday | Blue Escape | Seaside desk, blue glass and silver |
| Saturday | Lavender Lounge | Lavender ceramic, soft fabric, sleeping tabby |
| Sunday | Ruby Sunset | Ruby reading nook, sunset, ginger cat |

Each atlas contains sixteen semantic props in row-major order: dashboard planner, evaluation clipboard, cases folder, appeal letter, coaching notebook, library books, schedule clock, users, chat bubbles, settings gear, search magnifier, preview camera, save clipboard, export document, upload document, and security lock/key. Alpha bounds in `iconGeometry.mjs` center each prop without cutting off its shadow. Original SVG children stay in place until an atlas loads and remain available in forced colors or print.

The refresh changes presentation across shared tabs, cards, Case Information, Coaching, slide chooser and Login. Original component geometry, handlers, disabled controls, credential flow, stored IDs, Bangkok calendar transitions, Festival priority and KPI red/green remain unchanged. There is no new entitlement, quest or manual upgrade gate for 7 Days.
